This Privacy Policy explains what information Ashiko collects and uses to provide Discord server and creator tools.

1. Information Ashiko may collect

Ashiko may store or process:

  • Discord server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, and user IDs needed for configuration and interactions;
  • server setup choices, Builder settings, AutoPost routes, Backdraft review items, Hat Draw settings, and saved asset references;
  • creator links, platform names, post URLs, titles, thumbnails, timestamps, and related metadata used for AutoPost and Backdraft;
  • premium plan state, entitlement information, or manually assigned tier status;
  • basic operational logs needed for debugging, abuse prevention, security, and reliability.

2. Uploaded assets

If server owners upload GIFs or other supported assets, such as Hat Draw or Founder visuals, Ashiko may store those files or file references so the feature can work across restarts and deployments.

3. What Ashiko does not intentionally collect

Ashiko is not designed to collect private Discord messages, payment card numbers, government IDs, passwords, or sensitive personal information. Payment processing and identity checks, where used, are handled by Discord, Stripe, or other supported payment providers.

4. How information is used

Information is used to:

  • run Ashiko features;
  • remember server configuration;
  • route creator posts and Backdraft items;
  • apply Free, Plus, Pro, or other feature access;
  • debug errors, prevent abuse, and protect service reliability;
  • respond to support requests.

5. Sharing

Ashiko does not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when needed to operate the service, comply with law or platform rules, investigate abuse/security issues, or use infrastructure providers such as hosting, Discord, payment, or monitoring services.

6. Data retention

Configuration and feature data may be kept while Ashiko remains installed or while needed to operate the service. Operational logs may be kept for a limited time for debugging and security. Some backups or hosting records may persist for a reasonable period.

7. Removing data

Removing Ashiko from a server stops future feature use in that server. Server owners may request deletion or review of stored configuration data through the official Ashiko or Zenithoche support/contact path.

8. Children

Ashiko is intended for use through Discord and follows Discord’s age and account requirements. Ashiko is not intended to knowingly collect information from children outside Discord’s permitted use.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to protect stored data. No online service can guarantee perfect security.

10. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ashiko changes. Continued use of Ashiko after changes means the updated policy applies.

11. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, use the official Ashiko or Zenithoche support server/contact path listed in the Discord application profile.
